Case study

Inside the traffic surveillance system

One video stream in, six independent violation checks, and alerts out. Each module pairs a detector with its own decision logic.

Video stream YOLO detection Tracking Violation logic Plate OCR Alerts & logs
Module 1

Red-light violation

Tracks each vehicle across the stop line and checks the signal state at the moment of crossing.

YOLO · DeepSORT · HSV colour analysis
Module 2

Helmet compliance

Finds motorcycle riders first, then checks each rider's head region for a helmet.

Two-stage YOLO
Module 3

Speed violation

Warps the camera view to a top-down plane so pixel movement converts to real distance.

YOLO · bird's-eye-view transform
Module 4

Wrong-way driving

Compares each vehicle's trajectory with the lane's allowed direction of travel.

YOLO · trajectory analysis
Module 5

Plate detection

Custom detector trained on CVAT-annotated plates, with augmentation to stretch a small dataset.

Custom YOLO · Google Colab
Module 6

Plate reading

Benchmarked PaddleOCR, EasyOCR, TrOCR and fast-plate-ocr to pick the most reliable reader.

OCR model comparison
